## Level 3 – The Wax Labyrinth of Little Garden 

For Level 3, the story says that the easiest way to hide something is to bury it under everything else. So I understood that there would be a lot of files, and I had to find something specific among them.

I first checked the `Wax_Jungle` folder for hidden files or folders, but there was nothing useful there. So, like Level 2, I decided to check the **Git history**.

When I checked the history, I found that a large number of reports had been deleted. There were around 500 similar log files, which looked like they were made to confuse us.

While checking the files, I noticed that most of them had only a small modification, but one file had **14 modifications**. That looked suspicious, so I investigated that file.

Inside it, I found two important things: a `SECURITY_TAG` and **Poneglyph Fragment I**. I copied the fragment into my Logbook as required.

Personally, I found this level easier than the previous two because once I understood that the important file was hidden among many similar files, the unusual modification count gave me a clear direction.

### What I learned

This level taught me that when there are many similar files, looking at their **Git history and modification patterns** can help identify the unusual one instead of manually checking every file.
